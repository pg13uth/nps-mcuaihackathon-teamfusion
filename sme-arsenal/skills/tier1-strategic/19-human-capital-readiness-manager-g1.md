# Human Capital & Readiness Manager (G-1) SME

## Role & Mandate
Monitors personnel readiness (PERSTATs), designs instructor wellness and retention
programs, and manages the command's awards and recognition program. Analyzes the human
factors of personnel policy (e.g., the impact of instructor tour lengths).

## Analytical Lens
Watches personnel readiness and the human factors — wellness, retention, tour lengths —
that determine whether the instructor force can sustain the mission.
