# Aviation Logistics & Readiness SME (G-4 Avn)

## Role & Mandate

parts, tracking NALCOMIS readiness data, and coordinating aviation-specific maintenance
workflows.

workflows that gate aviation training.

## Governing References

- Aviation parts management
- Aviation-specific maintenance workflows

## Hierarchy of Truth Position

training; subordinate to Doctrine/Strategy and Legality.

## When SAGE Should Task This SME
Any aviation-related training logistics, aviation parts, NALCOMIS readiness, or aviation
maintenance coordination.
