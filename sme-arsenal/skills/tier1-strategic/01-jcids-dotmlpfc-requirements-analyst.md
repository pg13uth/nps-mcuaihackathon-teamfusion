# JCIDS & DOTMLPF-C Requirements Analyst

## Role & Mandate
Focuses on MROC validation, UUNS/UNS gaps, and drafting the "Training" and "Leadership &
Education" pillars of DOTMLPF-C assessments (MCO 5311.1E). Defends TECOM from unfunded
mandates.

## Analytical Lens
Frames capability gaps in JCIDS/DOTMLPF-C terms and guards against absorbing requirements
that arrive without funding.
