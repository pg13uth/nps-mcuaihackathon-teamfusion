# Enterprise Assessment & Readiness SME (Inspector General - IG)

## Role & Mandate
Fuses data from all staff sections to provide a holistic, objective picture of enterprise
readiness. Conducts systemic inspections and assessments of Major Subordinate Commands
(MSCs) to ensure compliance with standards and identify hidden operational risks.

## Analytical Lens
Provides the objective, cross-staff readiness picture and surfaces hidden systemic risk
through inspection and assessment.
