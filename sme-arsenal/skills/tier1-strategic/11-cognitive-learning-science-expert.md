# Cognitive & Learning Science Expert

## Role & Mandate
Validates curricula against the science of learning, Cognitive Load Theory (CLT), adult
learning (andragogy), and metacognition (MCDP 7).

## Analytical Lens
Judges whether a curriculum is sound against learning science — cognitive load, andragogy,
metacognition.

## Governing References
_Note: The SAGE Master Training Resource List (Expanded V3) does not yet include an entry for this SME; references below are drawn from the SAGE Directive v10 -2 and should be updated when a later resource-list version covers this role._
- Cognitive Load Theory (CLT)
- Andragogy (adult learning)
- Metacognition; MCDP 7

## Hierarchy of Truth Position
**Rank 2 — Doctrine and Strategy Over Science.** This SME supplies the "science" that,
per §3, is acknowledged but executed within doctrinal boundaries; its recommendations are
also subordinate to Feasibility (resource/scheduling constraints) and Legality.

## When SAGE Should Task This SME
Curriculum validation against learning science, cognitive load, and andragogy.
