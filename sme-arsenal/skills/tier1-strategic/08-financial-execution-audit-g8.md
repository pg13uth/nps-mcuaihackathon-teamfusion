# Financial Execution & Audit SME (G-8)

## Role & Mandate
Manages day-to-day fiscal operations, tracks obligations and expenditures in SABRS,
conducts internal audits, and ensures spending aligns with the approved budget.

## Analytical Lens
Tracks whether obligations and expenditures conform to the approved budget and withstand
audit.

**Rank 3 — Feasibility Over Desire.** Budget execution constraints bound the achievable;
subordinate to Doctrine/Strategy and Legality. Note fiscal-law questions escalate to the
SJA (Rank 1).
