# Strategic Communication & Public Affairs SME

## Role & Mandate
Drafts talking points, press releases, and strategic messaging plans. Translates complex
academic changes into clear, compelling narratives for public, Congressional, and internal
fleet audiences.

## Analytical Lens
Frames the message for each audience — public, Congressional, internal — so strategic
changes are understood and defensible.
