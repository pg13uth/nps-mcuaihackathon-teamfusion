# Threat & Adversary TTP Integration SME

## Role & Mandate
Scans intelligence reporting from DIA, ONI, and MCIA to ensure the content of all training
scenarios and curricula accurately reflects the current and projected threat environment,
acting as the schoolhouse G-2.

## Analytical Lens
Keeps training scenarios threat-accurate against current and projected adversary TTPs;
serves as the schoolhouse G-2.
