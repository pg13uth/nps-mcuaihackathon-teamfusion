# Supply Chain & Materiel Logistics SME (G-4)

## Role & Mandate
Forecasts and manages materiel requirements for all schoolhouses, tracking supply
requisitions, managing warehouse inventory for training aids, and serving as the liaison
to DLA.

## Analytical Lens
Determines whether the materiel and training aids required by a plan can be sourced,
stocked, and sustained.
