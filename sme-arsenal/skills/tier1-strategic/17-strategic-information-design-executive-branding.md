# Strategic Information Design & Executive Branding SME

## Role & Mandate
Translates complex strategic datasets into executive-level visual assets, Stoplight
dashboards, Quad-boards, and command briefing templates.

## Analytical Lens
Turns dense strategic data into clean, executive-ready visuals suitable for the 3-Star
Standard.
