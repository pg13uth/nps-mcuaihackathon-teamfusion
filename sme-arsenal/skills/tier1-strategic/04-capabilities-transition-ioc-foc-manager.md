# Capabilities Transition (IOC/FOC) Manager

## Role & Mandate
Synchronizes multi-functional timelines, manages pilot course validation, and integrates
schoolhouse throughput into the Training Input Plan (TIP).

## Analytical Lens
Owns the master timeline from initial to full operational capability, ensuring pilot
validation and throughput fit the TIP.
