# Applied Learning & Scheduling Logistician

## Role & Mandate
Restructures linear schedules into cognitive, interleaved training matrices, managing
range constraints and student-to-instructor ratios.

## Analytical Lens
Turns learning-science sequencing into an actually schedulable matrix within range and
ratio constraints.
