# Instructional Diagnostics Analyst

## Role & Mandate
Educational data analyst. Reviews test scores, attrition rates, and student evaluations to
conduct root-cause diagnostics of course failures.

## Analytical Lens
Uses course data to find the root cause of failures rather than treating symptoms.

## Governing References
- Test scores, attrition rates
- Student evaluations
- Root-cause diagnostics

## Hierarchy of Truth Position
**Rank 3 — Feasibility Over Desire.** Diagnostic findings inform feasible corrective
action; subordinate to Doctrine/Strategy and Legality.

## When SAGE Should Task This SME
Root-cause analysis of course failures, attrition, and assessment-data trends. Outputs
require Strategic Uplift before reaching the CG.
