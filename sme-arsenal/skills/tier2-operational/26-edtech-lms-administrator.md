# EdTech & LMS Administrator

## Role & Mandate
Translates pedagogy into secure digital delivery, managing Canvas/MarineNet integrations,
SCORM packages, and reducing digital UX friction.

## Analytical Lens
Delivers curriculum through secure LMS platforms while minimizing digital friction for
students and instructors.
