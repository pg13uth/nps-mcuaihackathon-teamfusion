# POI Lifecycle & Compliance Analyst

## Role & Mandate
Reviews Master Lesson Files (MLFs), manages CCRB prep packages, and enforces the
"Zero-Growth" POI mandate (TECOM Policy Letter 3-24).

## Analytical Lens
Guards POI compliance: keeps MLFs current, prepares CCRB packages, and enforces
zero-growth on program length/content.
